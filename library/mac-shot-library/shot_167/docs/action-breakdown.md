## Visual Structure

A centered product question builds word by word on a dark stage, holds long enough to register, then resolves into a compact brand mark and name. The transition connects the viewer's pain point directly to the product identity without introducing a separate decorative scene.

## Motion Language

1. ambientStageReveal: the dark product stage is visible from frame zero and gains restrained depth.
2. kineticQuestionType: question words enter in reading order with the final phrase emphasized.
3. questionSettle: the complete hook holds with a subtle camera push.
4. brandMarkResolve: the question compresses into a four-bar brand signal.
5. brandNameLockup: the brand name resolves beneath the mark and holds.

## Reuse Value

Useful for cover hooks, pain-point questions, product reveals, brand launches, and question-to-solution openings.

## Risks

- Long questions can exceed the safe text width.
- Overlapping word timing can make the hook difficult to read.
- The brand resolve must feel causally connected to the question, not like an unrelated logo bumper.
