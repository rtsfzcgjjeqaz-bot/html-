## Visual Structure

A four-quadrant gallery that showcases different product or content modes in a tidy editorial grid. The motion should feel curated and modular, not like a random collage.

## Motion Language

1. quadGridBuild: the four panels establish themselves in sequence.
2. panelImageLift: each quadrant gets a subtle independent rise to keep the layout alive.
3. gridDepthTilt: the whole gallery receives a slight depth tilt for polish.
4. panelCrossFadeSwap: selective panel emphasis rotates attention across the quadrants.
5. quadrantHold: the full grid settles for readability.

## Reuse Value

Useful for appGrid scenes, gallery overviews, multi-mode product surfaces, editorial product showcases, and feature collections.

## Risks

- Image-heavy panels can drift into poster collage territory.
- The outer grid must stay extremely aligned.
- Depth motion should stay subtle to avoid a PPT feel.
